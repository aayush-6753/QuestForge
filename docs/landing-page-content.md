## Direction

The hero already sets a strong fantasy and self-improvement direction. Keep the rest of the page minimal and build the story around:

```text
quests -> XP -> attributes -> progress
```

## 1. How It Works

### Every Great Journey Starts With A Quest.

Life RPG turns the things you already want to accomplish into a progression system that makes improvement visible.

### 01 - Create Your Quest

Turn a goal, habit, or responsibility into a clear mission.

Examples:

- "Run 3 km."
- "Study for 45 minutes."
- "Finish my portfolio."

### 02 - Complete The Mission

Take action in the real world and check it off when it is done.

### 03 - Earn XP & Grow

Completed quests reward XP and strengthen attributes like Strength, Knowledge, Discipline, Creativity, and more.

### 04 - Build Your Character

Watch your stats, streaks, achievements, and completed adventures become a record of who you are becoming.

## 7. FAQ

### Questions Before You Begin?

### Is Life RPG a game?

Not exactly. Your real life is the game. Life RPG gives your goals and habits the progression systems that make RPGs satisfying.

### What can become a quest?

Almost anything: workouts, studying, reading, projects, routines, personal challenges, or long-term goals.

### Do I choose my own attributes?

You can shape your character around the areas of life you want to develop.

### Do I have to be productive all the time?

No. The goal is not endless productivity. It is intentional progress toward the life you want.
